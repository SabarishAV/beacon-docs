About the Project

BEACON (Blind Enhancement and Assistive Cap with Optical Navigation) was developed as our college capstone. The system combines a wearable ESP32-CAM cap, a Python AI vision pipeline, a Fastify backend, and an Expo mobile app into one cohesive assistive platform.
